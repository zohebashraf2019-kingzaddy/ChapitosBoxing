Built in Reno.
Run like a fight camp.

Chapito's Boxing Gym is a nonprofit gym on West Moana Lane, opened in 2021 by Reno professional boxer Oscar “Chapito” Vasquez.

This isn't a cardio studio with a heavy bag in the corner. Every class teaches real fundamentals: stance, footwork, defense, and clean punches. The conditioning is the same work our amateur team does before a fight night.

Families train side by side here. Kids learn discipline, adults get fight-fit, and the ones who want to compete get a coach in their corner. Bring them on fight day and you'll see what that means.

The card

Pick your corner. Every program starts with fundamentals.

  1. Youth boxing
    Ages 6–12

    Footwork, coordination, respect and confidence. Kids learn the basics the right way, with coaches watching every rep.

  2. Teens
    Ages 13–17

    Structured training that builds discipline and real skill. A path to amateur competition for the ones who want it.

  3. Adults
    All levels

    Get in fight shape without guessing. Technique, bag work, mitts and conditioning in a 1-hour session.

  4. Women's boxing
    Fri 6 PM & Sat 8 AM

    An all-women's class. Burn calories, build confidence, get stronger, and have fun. No experience needed.

  5. Pro & amateur
    Fight team

    Competition prep for amateur and pro boxers: sparring, game plans and a coach in your corner on fight night.

First time?

2 free trial days for regular classes, 1 free day for the women's program. Monthly memberships and drop-ins available.

Bring

Boxing gloves, hand wraps and a water bottle. Wear clothes you can move in.
